🔒 Kid Lock

Tap 🔒 at the top to lock: Bumble goes full screen, the back button and grown-up buttons are blocked, and you hold 🔓 for 3 seconds to unlock. For a lock that also blocks the home swipe:
iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn on and set a passcode. Open Bumble, triple-click the side button, tap Start. Triple-click again to end.
Android: Settings → Security → App pinning (or "Pin windows") → turn on. Open Bumble, open Recent apps, tap the Bumble icon → Pin. To unpin, hold Back + Overview (or swipe up and hold).
